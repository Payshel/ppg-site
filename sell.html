Questions sellers ask

Frequently asked questions

Am I obligated to sell if I fill out the form?

No. Sending us your property details starts a conversation, nothing more. Any offer we make is in writing, and you can take as long as you need to compare it with other options.

Do I need to make repairs or clean out the house?

No. We buy properties as-is, and you can leave behind what you don't want to take.

How fast can you close?

It depends on the property and the situation. We'll give you a realistic timeline up front, in writing, and we won't promise a date we can't keep.

Who pays closing costs?

Any offer we make will spell out exactly who pays what, so you know your numbers before you sign anything.

Can I pick my closing date?

We work around your timeline wherever we can, especially when you need time to move.

Are you a real estate agent?

Our founder, Payshel Sharma, is a licensed New York State real estate salesperson. When PPG offers to buy your property, he is acting as a buyer for his own company, not as your agent. You're welcome to have your own agent or attorney review any offer.
